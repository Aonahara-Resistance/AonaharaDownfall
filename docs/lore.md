# World building and lore

## Loom

Loom are a tool used to store and manipulate life energy.

Loom by definition is: an apparatus for making fabric by weaving yarn or thread.
Someone who study life energy will be able to utilize life energy for all kind of things,
manipulate it into all sort of stuff, weaving the fabric of reality itself, hence the name.

Someone who've studied the life energy might have their own loom, whether they bought it
or make it themselves.

It captures and stores the energy from the surrounding area, and then the energy can be
released into whatever its user desires as long as they know how to of course.

However in most cases people don't use it to stores the energy since its capacity is very
small, instead they use it to manipulate the energy that's already out there.

In some region the usage of loom is heavily restricted due to its nature but in some it can
be used without restriction.

Loom is completely useless in an area that lacks life energy, and in an area with an
abundant amount its power dramatically increases.

People don't need to use loom to interact with the life energy, loom is a tool that help
greatly with it but is not necessary.

Regions with the purpose of making the most out of life energy usually given the green light
to go nuts with the loom in their production area by the councils.

## Craftsmen

Craftsmen are basically the Aonahara Members of this world.

Their primary objective is to turn the life energy produced by deities into something useful.

The craftsmen are currently led food, and will follow his order no matter how ragtag it would be.

The craftsmen are divided into 5 Roles:
Translators, Proofreaders, Subbers, Editors, Quality Assurance

The craftsmen spend most of their time inside the basement but sometime they do come out.

### Rough draft

So in this "alternate universe" human took the path of mind over matter. They found the
existence of the so called "life energy". The origin at this point is still unknown, they
just thought it always there. It's everywhere, from the air into every human body, and
after they study it, they found a way to manipulate the energy.

The energy can be transformed into any matter given enough time, knowledge and the density
of the energy itself. The need for natural resources soon decreased, eliminating most of the
world conflicts. Long story short, the world physiological and safety needs are fulfilled
but the needs for belonging, esteem and self actualization remains.

The whole world unified into a single entity, albeit there's only one country now, given
enough supports some group of people will be allowed to control over a region.

Later study discover that the life energy is generated by humans itself. The more positive
feelings they have the more energy they create, and human can only contain a little of them,
so the excess energy is secreted. Because of that the new civilization is based around
trying to utilize the "energy" to fulfill the remaining needs.

So there's this guy named Mikuri who just figured out how to make the bottom of the ocean
live-able using the manipulation of the life energy. Soon he gathered followings, and
proposed to create a new autonomous region which will be focusing on producing goods out of
the life energy around the ocean with the name of "awounapara". The councils approved it
with the following terms:

- population should be stable; meaning little death and little emigration
- produce goods at a stable rate

Mikuri recently discovered about something called deities. Some believe they are a very
inspired individual who used the energy to transcend and later will return to human hood
after the energy ran out or become uninspired. Some also believe they are a result of
extremely high energy concentration and will dissipate after the energy goes weaker.

Whatever the origin is, there's one sure thing: they produce a whole lot of excess energy,
just like a star, fueled by massive energy and radiating the energy back. However this
energy is a different kind. On its purest form only a trained individual able to comprehend
it, let alone do anything with it.

Mikuri himself has the ability to manipulate it, so he saw an opportunity. Leaded by himself
he gathered people which similar capabilities, which later called the royal craftsmen by the
locals. Their objective is to turn the energy from the deities into goods. The goods are far
more superior compared to any other autonomous region even tho production is sometime slow,
therefore bringing people in which make them happy.

The royal craftsmen is fulfilling their self actualization needs by doing the job, while the
citizen is fulfilling their belonging needs while also doing their own thing on the region.
This, this is Mikuri's recipe for prosperity. And prosper it did.

Fast forward a little bit, some stuff happened. Mikuri passed ownership, the name changed to
Aonahara etc, and we arrived at our main plot.

### The 25 days

**Day 1.** In the early days, we thrive on natural resources, quickly rising to the top of
the food chain. However, this way of thriving is flawed. We're not only consuming everything
below but also tearing each as a whole is about to change but something will *remain the same*.

**Day 2.** We discovered the existence of something that we later called the "life energy".
It's a non-baryonic matter and at first we thought that it **cannot** be aggregated into
another forms since it doesn't have the capability to lose energy efficiently which is vital
to form a complex structures. But being a human we thought it is just a mere skill issue and
start banging our head against the wall and hope somehow someway we'll make it work. And
work it did.

**Day 3.** With the energy within our grasp we are now able to materialize anything into
existence, but as with everything in life it's not that simple. A random Joe can't just
materialize a comically large spoon out of nowhere, that would've been funny but
`reality is often disappointing.`

This energy acts like the one and zeros of life. Yes it has the capability to form anything
you can ever think of but the process is super involved.

`A thing = domain knowledge + amount of energy * time`

In a way it is like how we've doing things. Farmers produce crops with his knowledge of
farming, some time and the resources needed. It's like even with a brand new shiny miracle
resource to play with we're bound to took the same path, industrialize everything and
squeeze every drop of milk out of it.

**Day 4.** One of the reason why the mass industrialization is not possible is because the
life energy is unstable and will quickly dissipate when remain unused, but the main reason
is because the way the energy is created: when a person is filled with *positive emotions*,
the human body produces said energy. The purpose is still unclear to this day but many
believes it is the thing which prevent someone from going hollow. However a single person
can only hold a little amount of it so the excess will be released.

Thus the new world was built around this concept, to make everyone live a better life,
whether its a greedy mega-corp or an evil government, they have to one way or another oblige.

**Day 5.** At this point nobody really know who Mikuri is but he's about to form one of the
most prosperosus autonomous region in the entire world. There's something called the
deities. A lot of thing is still unclear about them but one thing for sure is that they also
produce life energy, a different kind, which is arguably better but said energy is never
fully utilized since the quantity is low, mostly appear on the middle of the ocean and more
complicated to work with, none of which is Mikuri's problem.

**Day 9.** And with that his initial preparation is ready. It is now noon in late April,
Mikuri stand on top of a boat directly above his designated location. Without hesitation he
started his plan, he gathered the energy from the surrounding area using some kind of *tool*,
he shaped the energy into a big dome like shape. Of course it can't be seen but air behave
differently in the presence of large concentration of life energy, making it obvious. After
some time he did a slam motion with his hand pushing the dome down.

Now the dome is in the bottom of the ocean, the water inside the dome slowly getting pushed
out. Mikuri then created another bubble, this time it is a lot smaller and he placed it
around his head, creating some-kind of air pocket. He then descend to the bottom of the
ocean. Arrived at the bottom he then planted the first grass there, marking the birth of a
new autonomous region and he called it "awounapara". Now all he had to do is to do the
paperwork.

**Day 12.** After a long winded discussion with the councils they finally officially
recognize Awounapara as a legitimate autonomous region with Mikuri as its leader. This
process is usually rather quick, moreover, Mikuri isn't the first one with the idea so in
theory this shouldn't take that long, but emptying a part of the ocean just so he can get
more efficient energy input just make them question Mikuri's sanity. But then they rember
that in this world anything can manifest and let Mikuri have his ocean, as long as Mikuri
meet their *agreement*, they will let him do whatever.

Soon enough people starts to come over. Mikuri establish a mutual cooperation with the
Resort Beach which is another autonomous region with the same vision in mind, he built a
railway leading to Awounapara giving people access. All kind of people were coming, those
who just want to see the place for themselves and help build it and those who already know
about the deities, longing for greater connection. The latter people will become the greater
part of Awounapara which later will be called as The Royal Craftsmen.

**Day 17.** With the first Royal Craftsmen established under fooooooood's leadership
Awounapara starts to prosper, more and more people are coming to Awounapara. One of this
newcomer is Nom, he's currently a student on summer vacation, the news about Awounapara got
his curiosity and he decided to spend his vacation there.

**Day 19.** The uncommonly pretty scenery in Awounapara will help Nom breath a fresh air
after a stressful semester, furthermore he thought that he'll be able to understand Suisei
more, a deity that he is quite fond of. The place is rather warm for an ocean, even some of
the craftsmen greeted him when he first arrived. Despite its sky rocketing popularity
there's not a lot people who stay for a long time, maybe the idea of living underwater still
quite concerning for most people. Compared to the neighboring Resort Beach, Awounapara
population is minuscule. Because there's not a lot of permanent settler it's like a small
village where everyone knows everyone, soon after Nom too will get along with everyone.

**Day 20.** While Nom is enjoying her stay Mikuri quietly left, resigned himself as a leader
and transferred ownership to fooooooood's. Even some of the craftsmen don't know the reason
of why Mikuri left. For the record Mikuri just made a portion of the ocean bottom live-able,
convinced the council to let him govern the area, then when things start to come together he
leave without elaborating any further. Under the new leadership Awounapara is now renamed to
Aonahara.

**Day 21.** The Royal Craftsmen is in disarray under the new leadership, strangely enough
they're now better than ever so they just roll with it. After quite sometime Nom decided to
work on one of his goal which is to understand the deities more. Up to this point Nom has
been relying on limited publications about it, far from enough. There's no better place to
learn about them other than this place and so he begin.

Nom started asking people whether are they interested in learning or teaching about deities
life energy and interested they are. It only took a few days for Nom to finally organize the
whole thing then he proceed to made himself the """principal""". And the class worked, to
profound success. Everyone liked it, the students liked it, the teachers liked it and Nom
liked it. Soon the interest increased, even more students are joining in, and some of the
craftsmen are willing to teach, then Nom decided to continue this until the end of his
vacation.

**Day 22.** The day has come, today is the final day of Nom's summer vacation. It might be
short but it certainly was eventful, but it's time to go. Before Nom board the train,
fooooooood offered to fully compensate Nom if he decide to come back and continue the
"school".

**Day 24.** A few month have passed, Nom is now done with the school and is ready to go back
to Aonahara. Just like last time he boarded the train from the resort beach and off he go,
it's a decently long ride and his enthusiasm only make it longer. Once the train arrived Nom
went straight to the city, there he saw familiar faces from last summer. As he make his way
through to his place of stay Nom meet more and people who welcomed him back but strangely
enough he haven't met any of the royal craftsmen but he didn't think too much about it.

When Nom almost reached his destination someone approached him, he can't see who it was, he
thought that he'll just greet him, the figure stopped right in Nom's path.

"have we met before?" asked Nom

and that's the last thing he say before he fall unconscious...
