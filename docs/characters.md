## Nom Nom

### Combat spec

- **Character:** Nom Nom
- **Class:** Melee
- **Starter Loom:** Cum stained sword

**Base Stats**

- **HP:** 4
- **Damage:** 17
- **Stamina:** 3
- **Stamina Cooldown:** 3
- **Acceleration:** 60
- **Max Speed:** 110

**Starter Loom Attacks**

- **Light Attack:** Swing sword once, dealing damage to enemies.
- **Heavy Attack:** Spins sword to deal continuous damage to enemies.

**Skills**


*Standard Skill: Get excited*
Nom Nom remembers last night's talk about something lewd and starts to become excited, boosting her stats and allowing her to swing faster. In addition to this, her stamina cooldown is temporarily decreased, but she becomes unable to regenerate stamina for some time after the skill duration ends.
This ability lasts for 10 seconds and has a cooldown of 15.

*Ultimate Skill: Scrilspeokr*
Nom Nom starts hallucinating and sees her so called "family" attack enemies to aid her. After activating the skill, nearby enemies will have a chance of being attacked by her family member. If all four members of Nom Nom's family attack a single enemy, they will deal a large amount of damage based on the enemy total hp.
This ability lasts for 10 seconds and has a cooldown of 30.

*Starter Loom Exclusive Skill (Passive): Get horny*
Become even hornier, improving the passive effect of the cum stained sword. The passive starts to increase the range at which enemies get inflicted with coom. It also gains the ability to root and stun the enemy depending on the amount of coom they got inflicted with.
- **Blessing:** Comet
Increases invincibility time after getting hit by 1.5x (for example: 1 second of invic after being hit turns into 1.5 seconds)
+25 max speed for 2 seconds after being hit

## Emuwaa

### Combat spec

- **Character:** Emuwaa
- **Class:** Magic
- **Starter Loom:** Parseids Staff

**Base Stats**

- **HP:** 2
- **Damage:** 20
- **Stamina:** 3
- **Stamina Cooldown:** 3
- **Acceleration:** 50
- **Max Speed:** 100

**Starter Loom Attacks**

- **Light Attack:** Shoot rock shards that pierces through enemies.
- **Heavy Attack:** Emuwaa charges up raw life energy into her staff in order to unleash a big shark of rock that explodes upon contact with enemies.

**Skills**


*Standard Skill: Golden Shower*
Emuwaa conjures up rock shards using raw life energy and uses them to rain down on her foes. During the duration of the skill, rock shards will fall from the sky and damage enemies within its AOE.
This ability lasts for 7 seconds and has a cooldown of 10.

*Ultimate Skill: BDSM (Big Destructive Summon Meteor)*
Emuwaa unleashes her BDSM to destroy everything in her path. Due to the impact of BDSM on their lives, enemies hit will be stunned for 2 seconds.
"I will put an end to this pitiful world using this meteor of mine!"
This ability has a cooldown of 30 seconds.

*Starter Loom Exclusive Skill (Passive): Harpe's Full Power*
Emuwaa taps into the real power of the Parseids Staff unleashing its real power. Enemies near the character will be hit by rock shards from time to time.
- **Blessing:** Devil
The more health this character is missing, the more damage this character does with their light and heavy attacks. (Buff is based on the percentage of health missing. So missing 1 health at 4 max gives less than missing 1 at 3 max. But the more health you have the bigger the max buff is. As is the minimum buff.) If this character is at 1 hp their speed is increased by 50%
